About QuickNotes

Quicknotes is a note taking app for saving ideas and reminders, keeping your thoughts organized and accessible.

How to use Quicknotes

  1. Open QuickNotes and go to the Home page.
  2. Enter your note in the New note field
  3. Click Add Note to save it to your list
  4. View all your notes in the Your Notes section

Features

  • Quick note creation
  • View all your notes in the Your Notes section
  • Simple and easy-to-use interface
  • Keyboard shortcuts for common actions

Keyboard shortcuts

Action Shortcut
Create a new note Ctrl + N
View all notes Ctrl + A
save current note Ctrl + S
